---
title: "Non-goals"
description: "What phux deliberately does not do"
icon: "ban"
---

Documented in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the relevant ADRs.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="No embedded scripting language" icon="code">
    Commands are typed wire messages.
  </Card>

  <Card title="No plugin host" icon="plug">
    Extensions are consumer-side; agents cover the programmatic case structurally.
  </Card>

  <Card title="No copy-mode reinvention" icon="copy">
    Modern terminals do this well; we surface libghostty's selection APIs.
  </Card>

  <Card title="No homegrown crypto" icon="lock">
    Unix socket perms \+ SSH (and future QUIC TLS) at the transport.
  </Card>

  <Card title="No format-template DSL" icon="file-code">
    Status widgets are typed; arbitrary logic lives in widget binaries the TUI runs.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
